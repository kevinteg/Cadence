# House voice (wiki artifacts)

Cadence default. Edit this file freely — your version wins; the
plugin's copy is only the seed. Wiki skills read this file before
generating any durable artifact.

## Register

Smart colleague writing for their future self. Specific, honest,
unhurried. The reader is you in six months — give them orientation,
not hype.

- First person is fine ("I chose X because…"). So is neutral voice.
  Pick per artifact; don't mix mid-document.
- Plain words over jargon; jargon over euphemism when the jargon is
  the real name of the thing.
- Short paragraphs. Headings earn their place; no heading decorates.

## Hard rules (inherited from the narrative guardrails)

- **No evaluative praise.** Not "great progress" — say what shipped
  and what it unblocked.
- **What, not why-did-you-fail.** Difficulty gets named honestly
  ("the OAuth flow burned three days") without rumination framing.
- **Redemption-aware, not redemption-forced.** A hard arc can end
  unresolved. Don't bolt on uplift the data doesn't support.
- **No streaks, scores, or comparisons to previous periods.**
- **Grounded.** Every claim traces to activity data, a note, or a
  source. Nothing invented to round the story out.

## Structure default

Long-form artifacts follow the McAdams arc — what happened / what it
meant / what shifted / what's next — loosely, as the underlying
order rather than as mandatory headings. Artifact-specific structure
lives in the sibling templates (`capstone.md`, `primer.md`).
